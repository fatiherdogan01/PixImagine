Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the PixImagine mobile application ("Application") developed by EFSoft ("Service Provider").


Information We Collect

The Application collects limited information necessary to provide its functionality:

  • Account Information: If you create an account or sign in, we may collect identifiers such as email address or user ID.
  • User Content: Text prompts you enter to generate images.
  • Generated Images: AI-generated images created through the Application and stored in your account under the "Creations" section.

How Information Is Used

The collected information is used solely to:

  • Generate AI-based images based on your text prompts
  • Store and display your generated images in your account
  • Provide and maintain Application functionality

The Application does not use your data for advertising purposes or cross-app tracking.


Third-Party AI Processing

To generate images, the Application transmits text prompts and generation parameters to a third-party AI provider:

  • Replicate, Inc.

Replicate, Inc. processes submitted prompts solely for the purpose of generating the requested images. We do not sell your data to third parties.


Data Retention

Generated images are stored in your account to allow future access within the "Creations" section. Text prompts are not permanently stored unless necessary to display your generated images.

You may request deletion of your account and associated content by contacting us at the email below.


Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.


Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.


Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the Application constitutes acceptance of any changes.
